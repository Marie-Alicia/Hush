How it works

Listen, learn, and look after the whole family

Hush is a companion for the first months, built around two questions: what does my baby need, and what do I need right now?

First

It listens

Hush spots crying the moment it starts and ignores everything else. Only cries are kept; all other sound is deleted on your phone straight away.

Then

It learns your baby

After each cry you tap what settled them. Over a few weeks Hush learns your baby's own patterns and shows its best guesses, with the reasons behind them.

Always

It looks after you

Calm-down help at 3am, a safe way to take a break, fair turns with your partner, and support when things feel heavy.

Works with what you have

No new camera needed

Most smart monitors only work with their own hardware. Hush listens through whichever of these you already have.

Plaud NotePin S

A small clip-on mic about a metre from the cot. The clearest sound, and 20 hours of battery.

Best accuracy

Your iPhone

Leave a phone by the cot on charge. Nothing else to buy.

Easiest start

Your baby monitor

Connect a Wi-Fi monitor's audio stream, or put your iPhone beside any monitor's parent unit.

Keep your monitor

Use two together and Hush only raises a cry when both hear it, so you get fewer false alarms.

Honest about what it knows

Hush doesn't claim to translate cries

Research shows that cry "translators" built on thousands of other babies don't work reliably. Every baby is different, so Hush learns yours.

  • Guesses come with percentages and reasons you can check, never one confident answer.
  • It uses your baby's routine as well as the sound: time since the last feed, how long they've slept.
  • A cry that sounds very different from usual is flagged, with NHS advice, instead of being guessed at.

Looking after you

A calmer parent helps a baby calm down

Babies pick up on the stress of the people holding them. So Hush treats your wellbeing as part of settling your baby, never as one more thing to get right.

Calm Coach

60–90 seconds of slow breathing, grounding or kind words, spoken aloud so you can listen with a baby in your arms.

A safe break

When nothing works, Hush helps you put your baby down safely and step away for five minutes while it keeps listening. Based on the NHS-backed ICON programme.

Tag-team nights

Hush sees who has been up most and suggests a fair handover. It drafts the message; you send it.

Daily check-in

Twenty seconds on mood and sleep. If low days pile up, Hush gently suggests talking to your health visitor or GP.

Support when it's urgent

If something you write suggests you're in crisis, Hush shows Samaritans (116 123), NHS 111 and 999 straight away. This check uses fixed rules, never AI guesswork.

Safety and privacy

Built to be trusted at 3am

Only cries are kept

Your phone listens and throws away everything that isn't crying. Clips are deleted after 90 days.

Not a medical device

Hush never diagnoses. It points you to NHS advice and makes it easier to talk to your health team.

Checked by clinicians

Calm-down scripts and crisis support are written to be reviewed by perinatal mental health specialists.

Your wellbeing stays yours

Mood and check-ins are private to each parent, never used for advertising, and shared only when you choose.

Questions

What parents ask

Can Hush really tell what my baby wants?

Not on day one, and never with certainty. After a couple of weeks of you tapping what settled each cry, Hush starts spotting your baby's patterns. It shows how confident it is and why, so you stay in charge.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. Hush can listen through your iPhone or your existing baby monitor. The Plaud NotePin S clip-on mic gives the clearest sound if you want the best accuracy.

Is anyone listening to my home?

No. Sound is checked on your phone and anything that isn't crying is deleted straight away. Cry clips stay on your phone unless you choose to share one.

What if my baby's cry sounds wrong?

Hush flags cries that sound very different from your baby's usual ones and shows NHS advice. If you're worried about your baby, trust your instincts and call NHS 111, or 999 in an emergency.

What if I'm not coping?

You're not alone, and it's not a failure. Hush's Calm Coach and safe-break guide can help in the moment, and if you write something that suggests you're in crisis it shows Samaritans (116 123), NHS 111 and 999 straight away.
